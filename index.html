System workflow

Technology supports—not replaces—human judgment

1

Detect

Transaction data is compared with the customer’s normal payment patterns.

2

Confirm

A conversational assistant asks context-sensitive questions in plain language.

3

Pause

High-risk transfers are delayed before funds become difficult to recover.

4

Escalate

A bank employee reviews the evidence and contacts the customer when necessary.

Academic prototype disclosure

This website uses synthetic data and simulated AI outputs. It does not connect to a bank, process payments, provide financial advice, or make real fraud decisions.
